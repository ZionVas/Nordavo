# Roosterly help centre

This is the complete knowledge source used for customer replies. Do not add product claims that are not stated here.

- **Forgotten password:** Go to `app.roosterly.nl/login`, select **Wachtwoord vergeten**, and enter the account email. The link is valid for 60 minutes. If it has not arrived within 10 minutes, check spam.
- **Add an employee:** Managers use **Team → Medewerker toevoegen**, enter name, email, and contract hours, then select **Uitnodigen**. The employee receives an app-install email. Only active employees are billed.
- **Swap shifts:** An employee selects a shift and taps **Ruil aanbieden**. Colleagues with the same role may accept. A manager approves under **Rooster → Verzoeken** by default; approval can be turned off under **Instellingen → Rooster**.
- **Payroll export:** Approved hours export to Nmbrs, Loket.nl, AFAS, or CSV. Configure under **Instellingen → Koppelingen → Salaris**. Exports run per week, four weeks, or month. The three named integrations require Plus.
- **Plans:** Basis costs €4 per active employee/month at one location. Plus costs €6 per active employee/month and includes unlimited locations, API access, and payroll integrations. Prices exclude VAT. Upgrade under **Instellingen → Abonnement**; the new price starts on the next invoice.
- **Invoices:** Invoices are emailed on the first of each month and downloadable under **Instellingen → Abonnement → Facturen**. Company name, address, or VAT number changes for future invoices are made under **Instellingen → Bedrijfsgegevens**.
- **Locations:** Locations are included in Plus. Add one under **Instellingen → Vestigingen → Nieuwe vestiging**. Employees may be linked to multiple locations.
- **Languages:** Web and mobile apps support Dutch, English, and German. Each user selects a language under **Profiel → Taal**.
- **Data location:** Data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany) and processed under a data processing agreement, downloadable under **Instellingen → Juridisch**.
- **Feature ideas:** Ideas are read; feature requests are posted to `roadmap.roosterly.nl` for voting. Individual delivery dates are not promised.
- **Status:** Current outages and planned maintenance are listed at `status.roosterly.nl`.
- **Mobile requirements:** iOS 16+ and Android 10+. Location permission is required for location-based clock-in.

## Mandatory human handoff

Always hand off refund or disputed-charge requests, cancellations and contract changes, personal-data/AVG requests, possible security incidents, legal threats, and customers threatening to leave. Never repeat payment details in a reply or log. Customer replies must use the language the customer wrote in; handoff notes must be in English.
